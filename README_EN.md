## 5. Client Setup & Subscription Guide (Clash Verge, Sing-box, Shadowrocket)

### Windows & macOS: Clash Verge Rev
1. Download the latest installer from the official [Clash Verge Rev GitHub Repository](https://github.com/clash-verge-rev/clash-verge-rev).
2. In Clash Verge Rev, open **Profiles** on the left menu.
3. Paste your Just My Socks **Subscription URL** in the top bar and click **Import**.
4. Right-click the imported profile to activate it. Go to **Proxies**, choose **Rule** mode, and toggle **System Proxy** on in Settings.

### iOS (iPhone / iPad): Shadowrocket
1. Open Shadowrocket (available on non-mainland Apple App Stores).
2. Tap the `+` icon on top right, set Type to `Subscribe` (or scan the QR code from your JMS dashboard).
3. Paste the subscription URL, save, and turn on the main connection switch.

### Android: Sing-box or Clash Meta
1. Install **Clash Meta for Android** or **Sing-box**.
2. Add a new remote profile with your JMS subscription URL, save, and initiate connection.
